# Evidence and admission log: Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU)

## VERIFIED

- TMU course codes consist of a 3-letter subject prefix and 3-digit number (e.g. `CPS 109`, `ECN 104`, `ACC 100`, `MTH 140`). Sections use letters (e.g., `01`, `02`, `A`, `B`). Components include LEC (lecture), LAB (laboratory), and TUT (tutorial).
- Student schedule management is hosted in MyServiceHub (RAMSS) and integrates automatically with student TMU Google Calendars at the start of term.
- OpenStreetMap extract (`osm-tmu-2026-09`, retrieved 2026-09-24) provides 30 mapped campus building footprints, verified entrance nodes, and outdoor pedestrian pathways over connected path nodes across Gould, Victoria, Church, and Gerrard Street corridors.
- Factual building identity and codes include `SLC` (Student Learning Centre), `LIB` (Library), `TRS` (Ted Rogers School of Management), `ENG` (George Vari Engineering), `DCC` (Daphne Cockwell Complex), `KHN`/`KHW`/`KHE`/`KHS` (Kerr Hall), `POD` (Podium), `JOR` (Jorgenson Hall), `ARC` (Architecture), `EPH` (Eric Palin Hall), `SHE` (Sally Horsfall Eaton Centre), `MON` (Civil Engineering), `RCC` (Rogers Communications Centre), `RAC` (Recreation and Athletics Centre), `VIC` (Victoria Building), `SCC` (Student Campus Centre), `OAK` (Oakham House), `PIT` (Pitman Hall), `ILC` (International Living/Learning Centre), `HEI` (Heidelberg Centre), `CUI` (Centre for Urban Innovation).
- OpenStreetMap data licensed under ODbL 1.0 (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Factual building codes and names are cited without copying proprietary artwork.
- TMU branding: TMU bold blue / gold accent. Gapwise remains an independent open-source platform and does not imitate official institutional trademark graphics.

## INFERRED

- Students can export their timetable from their TMU Google Calendar (.ics) or paste schedule text copied directly from MyServiceHub / RAMSS / Visual Schedule Builder.
- Routing utilizes the outdoor pedestrian network and public sidewalks between campus facilities.

## STUDENT WORKFLOW

- Option 1 (Fastest): Export class calendar from TMU Google Calendar as `.ics` and choose it in Gapwise.
- Option 2 (Direct): Copy schedule text from MyServiceHub (RAMSS) or Visual Schedule Builder and paste directly into the Gapwise import box.
