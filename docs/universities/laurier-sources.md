# Evidence and admission log: Wilfrid Laurier University (Laurier)

## VERIFIED

- Laurier course codes consist of a 2-to-4 letter subject prefix and 3-digit number (e.g. `CP 104`, `BU 111`, `MA 103`, `EC 120`, `CH 110`). Components include LEC, LAB, TUT, SEM.
- Student schedule management is hosted in LORIS (Banner) and MyLearningSpace (MyLS / D2L Brightspace) which features calendar sync and iCal export.
- OpenStreetMap extract (`osm-laurier-2026-09`, retrieved 2026-09-24) provides 25 mapped campus building footprints, verified entrance nodes, and outdoor pedestrian pathways over connected path nodes across University Ave W, King St N, Bricker Ave, and Hazel St.

Peters Building), `S` (Schlegel Building), `DAWB` (Dr. Alvin Woods Building), `BA` (Bricker Academic Building), `LIB` (Laurier Library), `FNCC` (Fred Nichols Campus Centre / SUB), `M` (John Aird Centre / Music), `AC` (Athletics Complex), `CT` (Career and Co-op Centre), `AA`/`ACW`/`AE` (Arts Building Wings), `SSB` (Student Services Building), `SRB` (Science Research Building), `MLUC` (Martin Luther University College), `TA` (Theatre Auditorium), `DH` (Dining Hall), `KSR` (King Street Residence), `BR` (Bricker Residence), `WR` (Willison Residence), `LHR` (Little House), `MH` (MacDonald House), `CMH` (Claudette Millar Hall).
